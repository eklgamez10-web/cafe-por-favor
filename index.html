From our founder

More than just coffee.

As a woman, immigrant, and U.S. Army veteran, I've learned that every journey takes courage, determination, and a little bit of faith. Café Por Favor is more than just a coffee business. It's a dream built on hard work, passion, and the belief that anything is possible.

Every cup is made with love, inspired by my roots, and served with the hope of bringing people together. Whether you need a morning pick-me-up or a moment to yourself, I hope every sip brings you a little happiness.
